##PBTA Exploratory Challenge##

Your task is to:

1. Explore the available datasets
2. Select one pediatric brain tumor type ("CANCER_TYPE" or "CANCER_GROUP") that you find particularly interesting.
3. a biological or clinical question that can be addressed using the data.
4. the analyses necessary to investigate that question.
5. your findings and discuss their implications.

Possible directions (examples only)
These are examples and not requirements. Feel free to propose your own approach:

Identify biomarkers associated with outcome or tumor subtype.
Explore pathway activation patterns or biological processes.
Compare molecular characteristics between tumor subtypes.
Develop a predictive model (e.g., for tumor subtype, molecular alteration, prognosis, or treatment response) and evaluate its performance.
Apply unsupervised learning to identify novel patient subgroups.
Integrate transcriptomic data with available clinical metadata to generate new biological hypotheses.
Deliverables

Jupyter notebook (or equivalent) 

Short report/ presentation (3-5 pages)
